Customer Churn Analysis

 Project Overview

Customer churn analysis project to identify customers who are most likely to leave a company and understand the key factors driving churn.

The project uses Excel, SQL, and Power BI to clean, analyze, visualize, and communicate customer churn patterns.

 Tools & Technologies

- Excel — Data cleaning and preparation
- SQL (MySQL) — Data analysis and business queries
- Power BI — Interactive dashboard and visualization

 Business Objectives

- Calculate the overall customer churn rate
- Identify customer segments with high churn
- Analyze churn based on contract type, payment method, internet service, and tenure
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Provide actionable business insights

🔍 Key Insights

1. Contract Type

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71%, compared with 11.28% for one-year contracts and 2.85% for two-year contracts.

2. Payment Method

Customers using electronic checks have the highest churn rate at 45.29%.

3. Internet Service

Fiber optic customers have a significantly higher churn rate of 41.89%, compared with 19.00% for DSL customers.

4. High-Risk Customer Segment

The combination of month-to-month contract + electronic check + fiber optic service has a churn rate of approximately 60.37%.

 Business Recommendations

- Encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts through loyalty benefits or discounts.
- Investigate the reasons behind high churn among fiber optic customers.
- Improve retention strategies for electronic-check customers.
- Target high-risk customer segments with personalized offers and proactive retention campaigns.
